Privacy Policy

Modern Fulani
Effective Date: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or make a purchase from modernfulani.shop (the “Site”).

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through the Site, we collect certain information from you, including:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Payment details (processed securely by our third‑party payment processors; we do not store full credit card numbers)

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you access the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system. This is standard for website operation and security, not for tracking or analytics. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for tracking or advertising purposes. Only essential session cookies may be used to keep your shopping cart functioning; these expire when you close your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders (including sending order confirmations and delivery updates)
  • Communicate with you regarding your purchase or customer service inquiries
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations (e.g., tax or accounting requirements)
  • Protect the security and integrity of our Site

We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional content. If you receive an email from us, it will be directly related to a transaction or a support request you initiated.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for collecting and using your personal information depends on the specific context:

  • Contractual necessity: We process your name, email, and payment details to fulfill the contract when you purchase a product or service.
  • Legitimate interests: We process technical data (like IP addresses) to maintain the security and proper functioning of the Site.
  • Legal obligation: We may retain transaction records to comply with tax or other legal requirements.

4. Sharing Your Information

We share your personal information with limited third parties to help us operate our business:

  • Payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal) to securely handle transactions.
  • Shipping carriers to deliver physical products.
  • Service providers that assist with website hosting, order management, or customer support.
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside your own. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction. We take steps to ensure that any such transfers comply with applicable law and that your data remains protected, for example by relying on standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

6. Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Typically, we keep transaction records for the period required by tax and accounting regulations. If you contact us via email, we will retain that correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

7. Your Rights

For EEA/UK Residents (GDPR): You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent (though we generally do not rely on consent for processing)

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at the address below. We may verify your identity before responding. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You can request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, the purposes for collection, and the third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You can request the deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You can request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information for cross‑context behavioral advertising. Therefore, there is no opt‑out needed.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: We do not process sensitive personal information for purposes that would give rise to a right to limit.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. To submit a request, please email us with “CCPA Request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity by matching the information you provide with the data we maintain.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
